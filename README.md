# FITLOG -- Choose Your Workout. Transform Your Body.

## About My Project 

Discover exercises, build your workout, and stay on track. Explore a variety of exercises, choose your favorites, and save them for later. Create your own personalized workout collection and make every training session count.

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

  - Next.js
  - App router(Next.js)
  - Tailwind CSS
  - TypeScript (ES6+)
  - React-Toastify (NPM Package)
  - API for Data in JSON format

## ✨ Features

1. **Add Your Favorite Workouts** — Easily add any workout you want to your personal collection.

2. **Remove Workouts Easily** — Change your mind? Remove any workout from your collection with ease.

3. **Track Your Workout Stats** — View your saved workouts along with total exercises, estimated calories burned, and required workout time.

4. **Manage Your Workout Plan** — View your planned workouts, see the total number of exercises, and explore detailed workout information from the plan page.

5. **Clear & Helpful Notifications** — Get simple, easy-to-understand notifications whenever you add or remove a workout.
